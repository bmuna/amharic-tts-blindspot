Model: Lab-et/omnivoice-amharic (~0.6B)
Blind spot: same Amharic spelling, two meanings — stressed vs relaxed pronunciation
Note: not different spellings (e.g. አለ vs አለለ); the letters are identical

Dataset: 4 pairs x 2 context sentences = 8 sentences
Human refs: 8 recordings
TTS first pass: 8 wavs
TTS 5x stability: 40 wavs

Results:
- First pass switch success: 0/4
- 5x within-sentence stability: 40/40
- Context switch success after 5x: 0/4
- Default stressed: ዋና, ሰፊ
- Default relaxed: ገና, አያት

Conclusion:
The model consistently picks one pronunciation per spelling and does not switch stressed vs relaxed with sentence meaning.
